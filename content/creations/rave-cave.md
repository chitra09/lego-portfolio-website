LEGO Fortnite Rave Cave (77082) — a 1,963-piece build of the party island from the game, with a giant brick-built Cuddle Team Leader bear towering over the whole thing. A purple roller coaster loops all the way around the base, and inside the bear's mouth there's a DJ booth. The set also packs in a mining area, TNT and dynamite that really blow apart, and 7 minifigures including Cuddle Team Leader, DJ Yonder, Aura, Evie, and Funk Ops.
